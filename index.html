A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are a few helpful details for celebrating our special day with us.

Can I bring plus one?
While we would like to invite everyone, we Leandro and immaculate, carefully selected the guests we would like to be present to make our wedding solemn. We only invited you, our closest friends and family to be part of this special event. We have a seat exclusively reserved only for you because we want you to witness our Holy matrimony and celebrate with us as we enter a new chapter; however, if you need to bring a companion, please let us know and we may be able to book a space for the two of you. Please note that only two seats will be considered due to the limited number of guests and we'll consider it as long as there are still seats available. We request your response so that we can reserve a seat for you or a two.
Can I bring my child/children?
Please note that this will be an adults only celebration. Although we love your children, we regretfully cannot accommodate them at both church and venue due to restricted numbers of guests. Children of immediate and extended family only are allowed.
What time should I arrive?
We recommend that you arrive at least half an hour before the start of the ceremony to make sure everyone is on time so we can get the event started on time. Please be reminded that we will start the processional as scheduled.
Is this an unplugged ceremony?
This is not an unplugged ceremony wedding. You can still take pictures / videos during processional but we request you to please stay in your seat and no roaming around while the ceremony is ongoing especially the processional part. We also request to let our Photographers / Videographers capture every aspect of our ceremony because we would like you to be in the moment with us without the distraction of electronic devices.
Should I follow the dress code?
Yes please do. We would like to see everyone is a same level of formality that follows our dress code and motif to make our wedding look neat and eye pleasing.
Can I send a monetary gift?
Your presence is our greatest gift. If you wish to give a monetary gift, you may use the GCash QR code in the Gift section.
